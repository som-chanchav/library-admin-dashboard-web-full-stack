Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about borrowing rules and digital services.

❓ How many books can a student borrow at once?

Students can borrow up to 3 books concurrently for a standard duration of 14 days.

❓ What happens if a book is overdue?

A small fine of $0.50 per overdue day is applied automatically until the book is returned.

❓ How do I pick up a requested book?

Once your borrow request is approved, visit the BELTEI Library circulation desk with your student ID card.
